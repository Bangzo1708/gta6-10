Port Solano — a complete open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6: Rico Vega, an ex-stunt driver who owes $40,000 to the fixer Otto Vane, works it off across five missions in the sun-soaked coastal city of Port Solano. Drive and walk a living city with traffic and pedestrians, steal cars, dodge the police across a five-star wanted level with roadblocks and a helicopter, fight, and follow a live minimap and HUD. All art, story, characters, vehicles, sound and music are original; nothing from Rockstar Games. Runs offline in a desktop browser from static files, no network.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
